💡 How Scammers Trick People with Links:

  • Faking Bank Names: Scammers buy cheap domains like sbi-bank-update.xyz or hdfc-kyc-portal.top to make you think it's your real bank.
  • Look at the Ending (.xyz, .top, .buzz): Real banks always use official domains like .com, .co.in, or .org. They never use strange endings.
  • Never type passwords on links from WhatsApp: Always open your banking app directly from your phone's home screen.
