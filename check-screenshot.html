💡 Why Scammers Use Images & Screenshots:

  • Bypassing Text Spam Filters: Fraudsters often share posters or images on WhatsApp because basic SMS/chat filters look for typed text.
  • Fake Official Logos: They overlay logos of State Bank of India, HDFC, or Kaun Banega Crorepati onto fake certificates to trick unsuspecting victims.
  • Advance Fee Traps: Almost all screenshot scams demand an upfront "registration fee", "processing fee", or "customs duty" before releasing non-existent funds.
