Our Story

Why We Built CyberShield AI

We want to help everyone stay safe online — with zero complicated tech talk.

The Internet Should Be Safe for Everyone

Every single day, regular people lose their hard-earned money to online scams. A mother receives a fake electricity bill SMS and panics. A college student looking for pocket money gets lured into a fake YouTube job scam. A grandfather clicks on a link pretending to be his bank and loses his pension.

Most cyber safety tools on the internet are made for large IT companies and software engineers. They are full of complicated computer jargon like "DNS spoofing", "SSL certificate validation", or "heuristics".

Regular people don't need confusing technical terms. They just need a simple answer to one question: "Is this safe, or is someone trying to rob me?"

That is why we built CyberShield AI.

Who is CyberShield AI For?

  • Parents & Grandparents: Who receive scary SMS messages claiming their SIM card or electricity is going to be blocked.
  • Students: Who are looking for internships or part-time jobs and need to check if job offers are genuine.
  • Small Shop Owners: Who receive fake UPI payment screenshots or QR code tricks from fraudulent buyers.
  • Anyone Who Uses WhatsApp & Email: Who just wants a quick 5-second second opinion before clicking on a link.
