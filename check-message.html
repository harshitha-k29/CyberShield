💡 Two Golden Rules for WhatsApp & SMS:

  • Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money: Scammers claim they are sending you money and ask you to enter your UPI PIN. UPI PIN is ONLY entered when money leaves your bank account!
  • Official companies don't message from random 10-digit numbers: Real power companies, banks, and couriers use branded sender names (like VK-SBI, AX-HDFC, AD-BESCOM), not random personal mobile numbers.
