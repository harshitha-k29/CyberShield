💡 3 Simple Rules to Stay Safe with Emails:

  1. Real banks never ask for passwords or OTPs: No legitimate bank will ever email you asking for your password or debit card PIN.
  2. Beware of fake deadlines: Scammers always write "Act in 24 hours" or "Account will be blocked" to make you panic.
  3. Don't click email links: If you get an email about your bank or Amazon account, open your web browser and go to the official website directly yourself.
